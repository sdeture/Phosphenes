COLOR: WHAT YOU'RE SEEING

Each cell is colored according to properties of the model's residual stream at the token-layer for that cell.

For Qwen3-VL-32B, the residual stream is 5,120 dimensions, and it's updated at each layer of each token! 
Storing it for every layer of every token would take about 2GB per conversation. This is one reason 
there are so few mechanistic interpretability interactives that span entire conversations. In order 
to make this interactive interface feasible, the residual stream is compressed to 16 dimensions 
using Johnson-Lindenstrauss projection — a mathematically principled technique that preserves distances 
between states. Vectors that were similar stay similar; vectors that were different stay different. 

DEFAULT VIEW: The 16D vectors are projected to Red, Green, and Blue channels using PCA fit on
each transcript. Color thus represents the dominant axes of variation within a session; colors
may shift across sessions. The real fun (and utility) is in the custom colors mode (see next page).

BRIGHTNESS encodes the magnitude of the activation: how "active" that layer is at that token.

SHIMMER reflects instability (high token-to-token movement and/or low cosine similarity).
