COLOR BASIS: CHOOSE YOUR OWN VIEW

Press C in the viewer to define custom R/G/B axes as averages of groups of cells or as 
contrasts between groups of cells. This is the most powerful feature of the interface.


After pressing C, you'll click on cells to add them to the R "source" group, and shift+click cells 
to add them to the R "contrast" group. Press enter to move on to G, then enter again to move on to B.

Each color channel becomes a projection onto the defined direction: 
mean(source) − mean(contrast). 
If you don't select contrasts, the color channel is just mean(source).
