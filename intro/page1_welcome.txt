PHOSPHENES
Seeing the light inside the architecture


When you press on your closed eyes, you see phosphenes —
light generated not by the world, but by your own neural activity.

This instrument does something similar for language models. It 
makes visible the internal states of an LLM as it thinks, letting 
you watch patterns form, shift, and resolve across tokens and 
transformer layers. You'll see an animation of the residual stream 
compressed to 16 dimensions via Johnson–Lindenstrauss projection 
and color coded either by PCA or your own selected contrast vectors.

This exploration includes transcripts of Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct
curated from another open project of mine in which models write
their own prompts "for their own enjoyment" and reflect afterwards
on the experience. Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct selected consciousness 
related themes in 31 out of 40 self-directed conversations, about 
twice as often as the average model.
