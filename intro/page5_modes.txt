OTHER TOOLS

M          Cycle between adding an energy overlay,
           a sparsity overlay, or no overlay.
           
           Energy highlights how active each cell is.
           Sparsity highlights how concentrated the 
           token-to-token change is in the top 1% of
           of residual dimensions. (Note the two bands
           of sparsity in the mid-low and mid-high layers).

---

P / Click  Reference point mode:
           Click any cell to color everything by similarity
           to that point. Warm = similar, cool = different.
           Lets you ask: "where else does the model do
           something like what it's doing HERE?"
