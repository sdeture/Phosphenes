READING THE DISPLAY

Each column is one token. Each row is one layer.
Early layers at the bottom, final layers at the top. 

The bar at the top shows who is speaking:
blue = user        amber = assistant

The text panel on the right shows the conversation. 
If the scrolling text becomes distracting, you can 
hide it with the tab key.

---

BASIC CONTROLS
   Space        play / pause
   <- ->        step one token (when paused)
   up/down      speed up / slow down
   1-8          switch between sessions
   Tab          toggle text panel
   I            toggle inspector (hover for details)
